# Cast of Namma Bengaluru

Every talkable person with a stable explicit id: the same 130 ids and the same placements as Mumbai (`docs/CAST.md`), reskinned for Bengaluru. This file is the single source for the 130 Kannada conversations: the id, who the person is, where they stand, what they look like and what they are doing. All locals speak **Kannada** (`kn`); the foreign tourists speak **English** (`en`). Role keys in code are unchanged (for example `chaiwalaUncle` is now the darshini owner), only the look, name and conversation changed.

Source of truth for the looks: `src/worlds/bengaluru/people/castLooks.js` (named overrides by id), `people/roles.js` + `roles2.js` (role defaults) and `people/variation.js` (seeded citizens). Exact spots: `window.__bengaluru.npcs.npcs` (dev) or `src/worlds/bengaluru/population4.js` and `details.js`. Non-talkable background: the stray dog at the darshini, crows and pigeons, and the passengers painted in the Namma Metro windows (the first coach shows only women).

## The hero: Rohan

**Rohan**, 22, a tech intern from Delhi, new in Bengaluru and learning Kannada. NPCs may call him "Rohan" or "intern-u" (and treat him as a newcomer). He is clearly an adult, a little taller than the locals: neat black hair, a red-and-navy checked shirt, khaki chinos, white sneakers, a navy laptop backpack, his office ID badge on a bright blue lanyard, and a phone in his right hand. No NPC wears that combination (NPCs with office lanyards use orange, green, purple, red or teal straps and plain backpacks).

## What Bengaluru people wear

- Women: sarees (silk-ish bright blouses, gold or contrast pallu), salwar kameez, jeans with a kurti or T-shirt, office wear with a lanyard; **jasmine (mallige) in the hair** on about half of them, bindi, bare feet at temples.
- Men: **veshti** (white cloth with a coloured hem) or checked **lungi** with a shirt for elders, vendors and temple-goers; jeans and T-shirts for the young; office wear (long-sleeve shirt, chinos, ID lanyard) near the tech park, UB City and the metro; khaki for autos, BMTC and guards; white and navy for the traffic police; red shirts for KSR porters; orange T-shirt and bag for food-delivery riders.
- Kids: shirts and shorts, salwar tops, school whites and navy.

## Sites used below

Darshini square (the spawn square with Shankar Anna's filter-coffee darshini, the masala dosa cart, news stand, fruit cart, jasmine stall, rangoli, Kannada film poster wall, cricket gully); old pete houses ch1 to ch3 (Mangalore-tile roofs); the small temple (gopura-style shrine, coconut-and-flower stall, chappal stand, bell torana); Namma Metro MG Road station; Vidhana Soudha (steps, pedal-boat booth, photo tripod, BMTC bus stop, auto stand, junction); Bangalore Palace; KSR Bengaluru (Majestic) station forecourt; UB City and the Nandi bull; Attara Kacheri (old High Court) book stall; Lalbagh Glass House ledge; Ulsoor Lake (promenade, benches, jetty and pedal boats, lake-island temple, sugarcane-juice stall); the tech-park tower (gate, cab line); the lakeside park (churumuri and masala-corn stall, corn cart, coconut cart, bullock-cart ride, rangoli spot, tender-coconut stall, churumuri cart); the Hebbal-style flyover toll plaza. Places in brackets like `[50, -95]` are polar `[distance, bearing]` from the spawn.

Props that stand in for a Mumbai one: the Elephanta ferry booth is the pedal-boat booth, the tiffin pile is the food-delivery scooters, the horse ride is a bullock-cart ride, the sand-art spot is a rangoli spot, the Ganpati poster is the Rajyotsava flag poster, the Haji Ali juice centre is a sugarcane-juice stall, the chadar stalls are coconut-and-flower stalls, the stock ticker is the "Now hiring / Walk-in" board, the pav bhaji stall is the churumuri and masala-corn stall (a tray of puffed rice and corn cobs, no frying pan). The 20 multi-step roles (full dialogue files, the ones mapped to the 20 Mumbai multi-step roles) are marked **(multi-step)** in the role column; the other 110 get one short conversation each.
