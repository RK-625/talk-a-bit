# Cast of Mana Hyderabad

Every talkable person with a stable explicit id: the same 130 ids and the same placements as Mumbai (`docs/CAST.md`) and Bengaluru (`docs/BENGALURU_CAST.md`), reskinned for Hyderabad. This file is the single source for the 130 Telugu conversations: the id, who the person is, where they stand, what they look like and what they are doing. All locals speak **Telugu** (`te`); the foreign tourists speak **English** (`en`). Role keys in code are unchanged (for example `chaiwalaUncle` is now the Irani café owner Saleem Bhai); only the look, name and conversation changed. Where a role has no explicit override in `castLooks.js`, the row describes the role's own look in `people/roles.js` / `roles2.js` (seeded details such as shirt colour may vary a little).

Source of truth for the looks: `src/worlds/hyderabad/people/castLooks.js` (named overrides by id), `people/roles.js` + `roles2.js` (role defaults) and `people/variation.js` (seeded citizens: about 9% of women in a plain abaya and scarf, men in kurta-pyjama, lungi or shirts, white skullcaps on some men). Exact spots: `window.__hyderabad.npcs.npcs` (dev) or `src/worlds/hyderabad/population4.js` and `details.js`. Non-talkable background: the stray dog at the café, kites, crows and pigeons at Charminar, and the passengers painted in the Hyderabad Metro windows (the first coach shows only women).

## The hero: Sameer

**Sameer**, 22, a fresh engineering graduate who has just moved to Hyderabad to look for a job and is learning Telugu. NPCs may call him "Sameer", "abbayi" (young man) or "engineer garu" and treat him as a newcomer, encouraging him about the job search. He is clearly an adult, a little taller than the locals: neat black hair, a crisp pale-blue formal shirt with the sleeves rolled to the elbow, charcoal trousers, dark brown leather shoes, a wristwatch, a small olive-green day pack on his back, and a clear plastic file folder of resumes held to his chest with his left arm. No lanyard, no check shirt, no laptop bag and no phone in hand (all of those belong to Bengaluru's Rohan). No NPC wears the folder, the olive pack or that shirt-and-trousers pair (`variation.js` `notSameer`).

## What Hyderabad people wear

- Women: sarees (bright blouses, gold or contrast pallu) and salwar kameez, jeans with a kurti; **jasmine (gajra) in the hair** on about half of them, bindi, bare feet at temples. In the old city (Charminar, Laad Bazaar, the old-city houses) a few women wear a plain **abaya with a head scarf**, face open, shown respectfully and never exaggerated; the Laad Bazaar bangle seller wears a bright salwar and a coloured dupatta over her hair.
- Men: **kurta-pyjama** (pale kurta, white pyjama), checked **lungi** with a shirt for elders, vendors and stall holders, white crocheted **skullcaps** on some older men and stall holders (plain, never caricatured), white dhoti or panche at temples; jeans and T-shirts for the young; office wear (long-sleeve shirt, trousers, ID lanyard) near HITEC City, Cyber Towers and the metro; khaki for autos, TSRTC conductors and guards; a white shirt, navy trousers and white cap for the traffic police; red shirts for Kacheguda porters; red T-shirts and a plain red bag for delivery riders.
- Kids: shirts and shorts, salwar tops, school whites and navy.

## Vehicles, metro and signs (not talkable, for reference)

Autos: yellow body with a black canvas roof edged in a diamond trim and a black front cowl, a few green electric ones (reference: Commons "Auto rickshaw at Hyatt Hyderabad Gachibowli"). Buses: TSRTC / TGSRTC red-and-white ordinary buses and green-and-white Metro Express buses with Telugu + English route boards (reference: Commons "TSRTC Ordinary Bus At Wave Rcok Sez Hyderabad", "TSRTC's Metro Express bus"). White cabs with a "టాక్సీ / TAXI" sticker. Plates read `TS 09 ..` or `TG 09 ..` on yellow commercial plates. The metro train is white with a blue band and dark windows and a red-green-yellow stripe on the cab, lettered "హైదరాబాద్ మెట్రో / HYDERABAD METRO"; the first coach carries pink boards "మహిళలు / WOMEN" and shows only women inside. Delivery bikes carry a plain red insulated bag.
