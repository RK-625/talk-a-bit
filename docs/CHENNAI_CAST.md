# Cast of Namma Chennai

Every talkable person with a stable explicit id: the same 130 ids and the same placements as Mumbai (`docs/CAST.md`), reskinned for Chennai. This file is the single source for the 130 Tamil conversations: the id, who the person is, where they stand, what they look like and what they are doing. Locals speak **Tamil** (`ta`); the foreign tourists speak **English** (`en`). Role keys in code are unchanged (for example `chaiwalaUncle` is now the tea-kadai owner Murugan Anna), only the look, name and conversation changed.

Source of truth for the looks: `src/worlds/chennai/people/castLooks.js` (named overrides by id), `people/roles.js` + `roles2.js` (role defaults) and `people/variation.js` (seeded citizens). Exact spots: `window.__chennai.npcs.npcs` (dev) or `src/worlds/chennai/population4.js` and `details.js`. Non-talkable background: the stray dog at the tea kadai, crows and pigeons, and the passengers painted in the suburban train windows (the ladies coach shows only women).

## The hero: Meera

**Meera**, 26, a food blogger from Pune on a Chennai food trail, learning Tamil to talk to vendors. NPCs may call her "Meera" or "blogger-ma", offer her tastes and correct her Tamil kindly. She is clearly an adult, as tall as the local adults: black hair in a high messy bun, a short turmeric-yellow kurta with a white block print over blue jeans, white sneakers, a small bindi, a camera on a strap across her chest, a tan leather sling bag on one hip and a small red notebook with a pen in her hand. No NPC wears that combination (NPC women in kurtas use pastel tops, none with the sling bag, neck camera or red notebook; tourists carry a held camera and a backpack instead).

## What Chennai people wear (checked against real photos, see the report)

- Men: **veshti** (white cloth with a coloured or zari border) or checked **lungi** with half-sleeve shirts for elders, vendors and temple-goers; towels on the shoulder or wrapped round the head; jeans and T-shirts for the young; office wear (long-sleeve shirt, trousers, ID lanyard) near the IT park and the suburban station; **khaki** for auto drivers, MTC conductors, police and checkpost guards; khaki with a **white cap and belt** for traffic police; red shirts for Central porters; red or orange T-shirt and bag for delivery riders; the temple gurukkal is **bare-chested with the sacred thread**, a white veshti with a gold border and vibhuti.
- Women: sarees (bright blouses, contrast pallu), churidar or short kurta with jeans, office wear with a lanyard; **jasmine (malli) in the hair** on about half of them, bindi, bare feet at temples.
- Kids: shirts and shorts, churidar tops, school whites and navy.

## Vehicles and train

Autos are yellow with a black canvas hood (about one in five is green), MTC buses come in the classic yellow with a blue skirt and stripe, the silver deluxe with a yellow window stripe and magenta-red bumper, and the old cream and maroon livery; call taxis are white sedans; plates read `TN 01 ..`. The suburban EMU is cream-yellow above and bottle green below with red-brown doors and the lettering SR; the fourth coach is the **ladies coach** with a green-and-yellow hazard stripe and the lettering மகளிர் / LADIES, and only women are drawn in its doors and windows.
